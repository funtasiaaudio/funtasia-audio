The Studio

Everything you need to start recording.

Nyan Studio is a lightweight multitrack recorder built around a direct workflow: bring in your backing track, arm a vocal track, record, make simple timeline edits, then export the result.

Features

Focused on the recording flow.

Multitrack Vocal Recording

Record up to 8 vocal tracks alongside your backing tracks.

Simple Timeline Editing

Move, trim, split and delete clips with Undo and Redo.

Backing Track Import

Bring your music into the session and record directly against it.

Metronome & Count-In

Set BPM, use a bar or time ruler, and start with a one-bar count-in.

Session Workflow

Save and reopen Nyan Studio sessions in the .nyanstudio format.

Flexible Export

Export WAV, FLAC or AIFF at 44.1 or 48 kHz, with WAV up to 32-bit float.
