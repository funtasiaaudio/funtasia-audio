The Studio

From recording to stems, in one focused workflow.

Nyan Studio is a lightweight multitrack recorder built around a direct vocal workflow. Bring in a backing track, record vocals, move and edit regions, create loop ranges from the ruler, monitor through a dedicated vocal reverb, then export a stereo mix or individual vocal stems.

Features

Focused on the recording flow.

ASIO & Audio Devices

Select Windows audio devices including ASIO from AUDIO SETTINGS.

Software Monitoring

Hear your microphone through Nyan Studio while the recorded vocal remains dry.

Monitor Reverb

Add vocal-focused reverb only to the monitor path without printing the effect into the recording.

Region Editing

Move backing and vocal regions, split vocals from the upper region area, marquee-select, and glue selected regions.

Loop & Repeat

Drag on the ruler to create a loop range, double-click a backing region to loop it, and toggle repeat playback.

Track Controls

Mute, solo, rename and reorder tracks, with per-track volume and peak metering.

Timing Tools

Use 3/4, 4/4 or 6/8, BPM, metronome, one-bar count-in, horizontal scrolling, and horizontal / track-height zoom.

Mix & Vocal Stems

Export WAV, FLAC or AIFF, or render individual vocal stems using the current export range.

Session Workflow

Save and reopen work in the .nyanstudio format with a dedicated Windows session-file icon.

FAQ

Questions before you start.

A quick guide to what Nyan Studio does, how monitoring works, and what you can export.

Is Nyan Studio a DAW?

Nyan Studio is not intended to be a full production DAW. It is a focused standalone application for vocal recording, straightforward region editing, monitoring, looping, and export.

Is Nyan Studio free?

Yes. Nyan Studio is free to download and use on Windows.

Does Nyan Studio support ASIO?

Yes. Nyan Studio supports ASIO audio devices on Windows. Select your ASIO device from AUDIO SETTINGS.

Can I use a USB microphone?

Yes, if Windows recognizes it as an audio input device. Software Monitoring can be used when you want to hear the microphone through Nyan Studio.

Is the monitor reverb recorded into my vocal?

No. Monitor Reverb is applied only to the monitoring path. Your recorded vocal remains dry.

Why do I hear my voice twice while monitoring?

Your audio device may be using hardware direct monitoring at the same time as Nyan Studio Software Monitoring. Disable one of the two monitoring paths if you hear doubling or a short echo. Headphones are recommended for software monitoring.

How many tracks can I use?

Nyan Studio supports up to 8 backing tracks and up to 8 vocal tracks.

Can backing tracks be moved on the timeline?

Yes. Backing audio is handled as a region and can be dragged to change its timeline offset.

What vocal editing tools are available?

You can move regions, split vocals from the upper region area, marquee-select, glue selected regions, delete edits, and use Undo / Redo.

Can I loop a section while recording or editing?

Yes. Drag across the ruler to create a Loop Range, then use Repeat playback. You can also double-click a backing region to set its range as the loop.

Which time signatures are supported?

3/4, 4/4 and 6/8 are available.

Can I export vocal stems?

Yes. Vocal Stem Export renders the vocal tracks individually using the current export range and audio-format settings.

Which export formats are supported?

WAV, FLAC and AIFF are supported at 44.1 or 48 kHz. WAV can be exported as 16-bit, 24-bit or 32-bit float.

Can I save and reopen a session?

Yes. Sessions are saved in the .nyanstudio format and can be reopened later.

Does Nyan Studio load VST or VST3 plugins?

No. Nyan Studio currently focuses on its built-in recording, editing, monitoring and export workflow rather than third-party plugin hosting.
