01 / THE STUDIO, YOUR WAY

Change the atmosphere

Why FUNtasia Studio exists

Opportunity to learn should not depend on what you can afford.

For a young person making their first track. For someone balancing an everyday budget. For someone who could afford expensive equipment but simply wants to learn. FUNtasia Studio opens the same learning environment to everyone, free of charge.

It is an educational and experimental space for studying masterclass materials and discovering how production works — not a promise of production-grade reliability.

Different circumstances. Equal curiosity. Equal access.

UPDATES THROUGH BETA 1.1.2 / DEVELOPMENT HIGHLIGHTS

From chord ideas to smoother MIDI workflows.

  • New in Beta 1.1.2 — Automatic MIDI input enabling: Connected MIDI input devices are enabled on startup and when reconnected or powered on; inputs you manually disable stay disabled.
  • New in Beta 1.1.2 — Bounce/Undo safeguards: Completely empty MIDI regions are skipped before bouncing, and Undo restores selection more safely. The root cause of a previously reported crash is not yet confirmed.
  • Experimental Ruri-style chord notation: Extensions are expressed as degrees (9 → 2, 11 → 4, 13 → 6); for example, Cadd9 → 1^235 and Csus2 → 1^25. Available for exploration in the Key & Chord Guide, Scale/Voicing Browser, guitar fretboard and Chord Track, including input previews.
  • Piano roll & editing refinements: Improvements to MIDI note editing, multi-track and group operations, and velocity-based note audition with better note-off/gate behavior.
  • Mixer & device workflow: Add insert effects from the Mixer + control, access instrument interfaces from the Inspector, and reorder VST device cards.
  • Stability & focus refinements: Plug-in scanning no longer forces its window to stay on top; envelope and voice-stop behavior has been refined.
  • Voicing auditions: Hear chord voicings using the bundled FreePats guitar and concert-harp SFZ instruments.
  • 300 original one-bar drum fills: GM-compatible MIDI patterns covering 200 in 4/4 and 100 in 6/8.
  • Drum preview sounds: Choose Acoustic Lite or Electronic SFZ sounds and audition MIDI fills from the Sample Library Browser or FILES tab.

Ruri-style notation is an experimental introduction. The Bounce/Undo safeguards are not a guarantee against all crashes. This remains a learning-oriented pre-release, and features may continue to evolve.
