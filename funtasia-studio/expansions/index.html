02 / GET CREATIVE

A simple workflow

Make the rhythm yours.

  1. Extract the ZIP and open the MIDI folder.
  2. Add a MIDI/instrument track in FUNtasia Studio or another compatible DAW and load a GM-mapped drum instrument.
  3. Drag or import a .mid file, set your own project tempo, then edit notes and velocities as you like.
  4. Use a Fill to replace part of a Groove: the final one or two bars for Epic Metal, or bar four (one bar) for Fantasy Folk. Avoid overlapping two full performances accidentally.
