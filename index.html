Soft Clipping · Hardstyle Sound Design

FUN Clip Distortion

Shape the clip. Sculpt the harmonics.

Inspired by Clip Distortion in Apple's Logic Pro, a familiar sound-shaping tool in hardstyle kick production. Hardstyle producers have used distortion and EQ in successive stages to sculpt the punch, pitch and aggressive harmonics of their kicks. FUN Clip Distortion takes inspiration from that creative workflow, not from Apple's DSP code.

Our independently developed clipping engine lets you shape DRIVE, a variable soft KNEE and positive/negative SYMMETRY. A TONE high-pass filter, a dedicated CLIP FILTER low-pass, and an additional LP FILTER switchable between PRE and POST positions give you control over what enters the clipping stage and what comes out. HP, Clip LP and main LP each offer 6 / 12 / 18 / 24 dB/oct slopes; a HIGH SHELF adds further tone shaping.

Choose NOS, 2×, 4×, 6× or 8× oversampling and Minimum/Linear Phase anti-aliasing filtering. A switchable DC filter, OUTPUT gain and parallel DRY/WET MIX complete the design. At MIX 0%, the dry signal is not colored by the effect filters (OUTPUT gain still applies).

Inspired by Logic Pro's Clip Distortion and its use in hardstyle kick sound design. This is an original FUNtasia Audio instrument—not an algorithmic clone. FUNtasia Audio is not affiliated with or endorsed by Apple.

Curve Distortion

FUN WaveShaper

Draw the curve. Shape the distortion.

Inspired by the intuitive transfer-curve editing of Image-Line's Fruity WaveShaper, FUN WaveShaper is an independently developed take on graphical waveshaping. Add nodes, bend curves, and create your own saturation, clipping, and wavefolding responses.

Its internal signal path uses 64-bit floating-point calculations for curve processing and oversampling, including when the host supplies 32-bit float audio. Minimum/Linear Phase choices, oversampling, PRE/POST gain, and Dry/Wet mixing let you decide how the effect behaves.

Inspired by Fruity WaveShaper's editing concept; independently developed by FUNtasia Audio. Not affiliated with Image-Line.

Quality Assurance

Carefully Built. Thoroughly Tested.

Great sound deserves reliable tools. Our development process combines standardized validation with dedicated tests shaped around each plugin's architecture.

01

Plugin Validation

We use pluginval to help identify stability, compatibility, and state-management issues.

02

Dedicated Stress Testing

Custom test scenarios examine DSP behavior, rapid parameter changes, and demanding edge cases.

03

Automation & PDC Testing

Targeted checks cover automation, processing transitions, latency reporting, and host delay compensation.

Test coverage varies by plugin, version, and applicable features.

Our Story

A world of its own.

FUNtasia Audio began with a question: what happens when an audio tool is designed from the beginning for a digital environment?

We take inspiration from the way sound behaves, then follow the possibilities of digital design. Each instrument starts with a clear idea of what it should do and how it should feel to use.

Plugins, applications, Rack, presets, music, moving images, and the website are all parts of one imagined world. The instruments are made to work in real sessions, giving you a place to begin and room to shape what comes next.

Instead of treating every change in taste or workflow as a reason to replace a tool, we leave carefully chosen parts of an instrument open to you. Enough room to personalize its character, without turning the plugin into a development environment.

We are building another choice: a growing collection of tools with their own character, purpose, and place in the world of FUNtasia.

A Note From The Developer

A chance to create,
for everyone.

I have a developmental disability and related sensory sensitivities.

As a student, I endured severe bullying while working to afford sound libraries and music equipment. Those experiences still affect me deeply, and I am currently receiving treatment for depression.

Having faced these difficulties myself, there is something I sincerely hope for.

A world where everyone has the chance to live as themselves and explore their potential, regardless of disability, financial circumstances, upbringing, or the challenges they face.

To make music. To express something of their own.

I do not want people to have to give up on that possibility because they cannot afford the tools.

That belief is why I devote special care and effort to developing free plugins at FUNtasia Audio.

To me, a free product is not worth less than a paid one. I take the quality and the breadth of our free offerings just as seriously.

To be honest, the time and costs going into development are currently far out of balance with the revenue it brings in.

Even so, I do not want to let go of this belief by reducing the number of free products or compromising their quality.

That is why I am actively asking for support from those who believe in FUNtasia Audio and are in a position to help.

Our Amazon Wish List includes development equipment as well as food and everyday essentials that help sustain this work.

Your support makes a real difference in keeping development going and bringing creative tools to more people.
